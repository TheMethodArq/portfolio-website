## Local Search Changed When AI Got Involved

"Near me" searches used to return a map and 10 blue links. You optimized your Google Business listing, built some local citations, and competed for the map pack.

Now people ask ChatGPT, Claude, or Perplexity: "What's the best HVAC company in Scottsdale for a 2,500 square foot home?"

AI doesn't show a map. It recommends specific businesses based on its understanding of quality, fit, and relevance.

**Local ASO is different than local SEO. Let's talk about what actually works.**

## How AI Handles Local Business Discovery

### What AI Knows About Your Business

**Information AI has access to**:
- Your website content (if in training data or searchable)
- Google Business Profile and other directory listings
- Reviews across platforms
- Media mentions and articles about you
- Social media presence
- Industry directories and associations

**Information AI infers**:
- Geographic area you serve (from address, content, service area mentions)
- Business size and type (from team size, about page, project descriptions)
- Expertise and specialization (from content topics and depth)
- Quality and reputation (from review patterns and third-party validation)

### How AI Decides to Recommend Local Businesses

**Query pattern**: "Find me a [type] business in [location] for [need]"

**AI evaluation process** (inferred from behavior):
1. Identify businesses in geographic area
2. Match business type and specialization to need
3. Assess quality based on reviews, content, third-party validation
4. Consider context clues (size of project, specific requirements)
5. Recommend best fit with explanation

**Unlike traditional local SEO**: AI isn't just matching keywords and proximity. It's evaluating genuine fit based on comprehensive information.

## The Local ASO Strategy

### 1. Google Business Profile Optimization (Foundation)

**This is non-negotiable for local visibility.**

**Complete every section thoroughly**:

**Business information**:
- Exact business name (consistent everywhere)
- Full address (if you have a physical location)
- Service area (specific cities/neighborhoods you serve)
- Phone number (consistent across all platforms)
- Website (with properly optimized landing page)
- Business hours (including holiday hours, special hours)

**Business description** (write for AI extraction):

❌ Not this:
"We're a family-owned business providing quality service since 1985. Customer satisfaction is our priority."

✅ This:
"We're an HVAC contractor serving Phoenix's East Valley—specifically Scottsdale, Tempe, Mesa, and Chandler. We handle residential AC repair, system replacement, and maintenance for homes 1,500-5,000 square feet. Our team of 8 certified technicians averages 15+ years experience. Licensed, bonded, and insured (AZ ROC#123456). Same-day emergency service available."

**Services** (be specific):
- Not just "Plumbing" but "Emergency Leak Repair," "Water Heater Installation," "Drain Cleaning," "Bathroom Remodeling," "Fixture Replacement"
- Each service as separate entry
- Include common problems you solve

**Photos** (show your actual work):
- Your team (real people, not stock photos)
- Completed projects (before/after)
- Your vehicles and equipment
- Your shop/office if relevant
- Recent work (dated if possible)

**Posts** (regular updates signal activity):
- Monthly minimum
- Recent projects completed
- Seasonal tips or reminders
- Special offers if applicable
- Company news and updates

**Reviews** (quality and recency matter):
- Actively request reviews from satisfied customers
- Respond to ALL reviews (positive and negative)
- Address specific points in review responses
- Recent reviews signal ongoing quality

### 2. Local Content Strategy

**Create content that demonstrates local expertise.**

**Location-specific service pages**:

**Not generic**: "We serve the Phoenix area"

**Specific and useful**:
```
## HVAC Service in Scottsdale, AZ

Scottsdale's extreme summer heat (regularly 110°F+) puts unique stress on AC systems.
We've serviced 200+ Scottsdale homes over 15 years and understand the specific
challenges of cooling desert properties, especially in neighborhoods like:

- Old Town Scottsdale (older homes, often single-pane windows)
- North Scottsdale (larger homes, 3,000-5,000 sq ft)
- Grayhawk (newer construction with modern HVAC)
- McDowell Mountain Ranch (master-planned, specific builder systems)

Common Scottsdale HVAC issues we address:
- AC units undersized for Phoenix heat
- Ductwork in hot attics losing efficiency
- Dust and debris from desert environment
- UV damage to outdoor units
- Monsoon damage and flooding concerns

Average Scottsdale service costs:
- AC repair: $200-$800
- System replacement: $8,000-$15,000 (depending on size)
- Annual maintenance: $150-$250

We serve Scottsdale from our Tempe shop—usually 30-45 minute response time
for emergency calls.
```

**This content**:
- Demonstrates genuine local knowledge
- Addresses specific local conditions
- Names actual neighborhoods
- Provides local-specific pricing
- Shows understanding of area challenges

**AI can extract and understand this as local expertise.**

**Local case studies and examples**:

Don't just say "we serve Phoenix." Show it:
- "How We Helped a Scottsdale Family Reduce Cooling Costs by 30%"
- "Emergency AC Repair in Gilbert: From 90° House to Cool in 3 Hours"
- "Replacing a 20-Year-Old HVAC System in a Chandler Ranch Home"

**Include specifics**:
- Actual neighborhood or part of city
- Specific challenges of that location
- Real timeline and costs
- Photos if possible

**Local problem-solving content**:
- "Dealing with Phoenix Summer Heat: When to Repair vs. Replace Your AC"
- "Mesa Homeowners: How to Know If Your AC Can Handle Phoenix Summers"
- "Scottsdale Dust and Desert Debris: Protecting Your HVAC System"

**Shows you understand local context in depth.**

### 3. Local Authority Signals

**Help AI understand you're established locally.**

**Local business associations**:
- Chamber of Commerce membership
- Better Business Bureau accreditation
- Industry associations (with local chapter)
- Community involvement

**Local media mentions**:
- Local news features
- Local business publications
- Community newspaper mentions
- Local blog coverage

**Local partnerships and relationships**:
- Preferred vendor for local builders
- Partnership with local businesses
- Referral relationships documented
- Community sponsorships

**All of this creates local authority signals AI recognizes.**

### 4. Geographic Relevance Throughout Site

**Make your location and service area obvious.**

**Homepage** (above fold):
- Clear statement of primary service area
- City/region prominently displayed
- Phone with local area code

**Footer** (every page):
- Full address
- Service area list
- Map or link to locations page

**About page**:
- How long you've served the area
- Team members with local backgrounds
- Community involvement
- Local knowledge and expertise

**Contact page**:
- Physical address if applicable
- Service area map
- Response time by area if relevant

**Consistent NAP** (Name, Address, Phone) everywhere:
- Website (every page footer)
- Google Business Profile
- Yelp, Facebook, other directories
- Industry-specific sites
- Social media profiles

**Inconsistency confuses AI and hurts visibility.**

### 5. Review Strategy for AI Visibility

**Reviews influence AI recommendations significantly.**

**What matters**:
- Overall rating (4.0+ minimum, ideally 4.5+)
- Number of reviews (more = better, 50+ is good baseline)
- Recency (reviews in past 3 months show current quality)
- Detail in reviews (specific experiences matter more than "great service!")
- Response rate (responding shows engagement)

**How to build review presence**:

**Ask systematically**:
- Email/text after every completed job
- Make it easy (direct links to review platforms)
- Ask satisfied customers specifically
- Follow up on positive feedback

**Respond to every review**:
- Thank positive reviews, mention specifics from their review
- Address negative reviews professionally, offer to resolve
- Show personality and care in responses

**Encourage detail in reviews**:
"We'd love to hear about your experience! Specifically:
- What problem were we solving?
- How did the project go?
- How's the solution working for you?"

**Detailed reviews help AI understand what you're good at.**

### 6. Schema Markup for Local Businesses

**Help AI extract correct local information.**

**Implement LocalBusiness schema**:
```json
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "LocalBusiness",
  "name": "Phoenix HVAC Pros",
  "image": "...",
  "address": {
    "@type": "PostalAddress",
    "streetAddress": "123 Main St",
    "addressLocality": "Tempe",
    "addressRegion": "AZ",
    "postalCode": "85281"
  },
  "areaServed": [
    {"@type": "City", "name": "Scottsdale"},
    {"@type": "City", "name": "Tempe"},
    {"@type": "City", "name": "Mesa"},
    {"@type": "City", "name": "Chandler"}
  ],
  "telephone": "+1-480-555-0123",
  "openingHours": "Mo-Fr 07:00-19:00, Sa 08:00-16:00",
  "priceRange": "$$"
}
```

**This ensures AI can accurately extract**:
- Your location
- Service area
- Hours
- Contact information

## Testing Your Local AI Visibility

**Weekly test routine**:

**Ask AI platforms local questions**:
1. "What [your business type] should I use in [your city]?"
2. "Find me a [business type] in [neighborhood] for [specific need]"
3. "Compare [your business] to [local competitor]"
4. "Is [your business] good for [specific local use case]?"
5. "What does [service] cost in [your city]?"

**Test across**:
- ChatGPT
- Claude
- Perplexity (particularly good for local)
- Google AI features

**Document**:
- Are you mentioned?
- Is location information correct?
- Do you appear for relevant local queries?
- How do you compare to local competitors?
- What information is AI citing about you?

## Local ASO vs. Traditional Local SEO

| Aspect | Traditional Local SEO | Local ASO |
|--------|----------------------|-----------|
| **Optimization target** | Google Maps, local pack | AI understanding of your local business |
| **Key tactic** | Citations and backlinks | Comprehensive local content and reviews |
| **Content focus** | Keyword optimization | Demonstrating local expertise |
| **Success metric** | Map pack ranking | AI recommendation frequency |
| **Timeline** | 3-6 months | 6-12 months |

**You need both, but they're different strategies.**
