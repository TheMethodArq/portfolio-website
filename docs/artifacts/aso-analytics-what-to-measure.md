## Traditional SEO Metrics Are the Wrong Lens

For 20 years, we measured search success with:
- Keyword rankings
- Organic search traffic
- Backlink count and domain authority
- Page views and bounce rate
- Time on site

**These metrics don't capture what matters for AI search.**

AI doesn't rank pages by keywords. It doesn't count backlinks. Your domain authority is irrelevant to ChatGPT's recommendations.

**You need different metrics to understand AI search performance. Here's what actually matters.**

## The Four Categories of ASO Metrics

### Category 1: AI Visibility Metrics

**What they measure**: Whether AI knows about you and mentions you when relevant.

### Category 2: Context Quality Metrics

**What they measure**: The situations where AI recommends you and positioning accuracy.

### Category 3: Traffic & Conversion Metrics

**What they measure**: Whether AI visibility turns into business value.

### Category 4: Content Performance Metrics

**What they measure**: Which content AI extracts and cites most effectively.

**Let's break down each category with specific metrics and how to track them.**

## Category 1: AI Visibility Metrics

### Metric 1.1: Mention Frequency

**What it is**: How often AI mentions your business when asked relevant questions.

**How to measure**:
1. Create 10 core questions relevant to your business
2. Ask each question to ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity weekly
3. Track: mentioned vs. not mentioned

**Example tracking**:
| Question | ChatGPT | Claude | Perplexity | Mentioned? |
|----------|---------|--------|------------|------------|
| "What CRM for 40-person consulting firm?" | Yes | Yes | Yes | 3/3 |
| "Custom development vs. SaaS for mid-market?" | No | Yes | Yes | 2/3 |
| "Technology consultant for manufacturing company?" | No | No | Yes | 1/3 |

**What good looks like**:
- Month 1: 2-3 mentions out of 10 questions
- Month 3: 4-6 mentions out of 10 questions
- Month 6: 6-8 mentions out of 10 questions
- Month 12: 8-10 mentions out of 10 questions

**Why it matters**: If AI doesn't mention you, nothing else matters.

### Metric 1.2: Competitive Mention Share

**What it is**: When AI mentions businesses in your category, how often is yours included?

**How to measure**:
1. Ask 5 competitive questions weekly
2. Track all businesses AI mentions
3. Calculate your share of mentions

**Example**:
"What technology consultant for 50-person business?"

AI mentions: Competitor A, Competitor B, Your Business, Competitor C

Your share: 25% (1 of 4)

**Track over time**:
- Are you gaining share of mentions?
- Which competitors are mentioned more?
- What questions do you lose mentions on?

**Why it matters**: Being mentioned alongside stronger competitors validates your positioning.

### Metric 1.3: Information Accuracy

**What it is**: When AI mentions you, is the information correct?

**How to measure**:
1. Review all AI mentions of your business
2. Check accuracy of:
   - What you do
   - Who you serve
   - Pricing/cost ranges
   - Location/service area
   - Key differentiation

**Score**:
- Fully accurate: 1 point
- Partially accurate: 0.5 points
- Inaccurate: 0 points

**Example**:
Week 1: 4 mentions, scores: 1, 0.5, 1, 0.5 = 3/4 = 75% accuracy

**What good looks like**:
- Month 1: 50-60% accuracy (AI learning)
- Month 3: 70-80% accuracy (improving)
- Month 6+: 85-95% accuracy (strong signal)

**Why it matters**: Inaccurate mentions hurt more than no mentions.

### Metric 1.4: Citation Frequency (Perplexity-specific)

**What it is**: How often Perplexity cites your content as a source.

**How to measure**:
1. Ask 10 questions relevant to your domain on Perplexity
2. Check if your content is cited in sources
3. Track citation frequency

**Example**:
10 questions asked, your content cited in sources for 3 responses = 30% citation rate

**Track**:
- Which content gets cited most?
- For what types of queries?
- How does citation frequency change over time?

**Why it matters**: Citations = direct traffic + authority signal.

## Category 2: Context Quality Metrics

### Metric 2.1: Positioning Accuracy

**What it is**: Does AI understand who you serve and what makes you different?

**How to measure**:
Review AI mentions and score positioning accuracy:

**Who you serve**:
- ✅ Accurate: "Thalamix serves 10-100 person businesses..."
- ⚠️ Partial: "Thalamix is a technology consultant..."
- ❌ Wrong: "Thalamix is an enterprise software company..."

**Differentiation**:
- ✅ Accurate: "Unlike traditional consultants, Thalamix provides fixed-price..."
- ⚠️ Partial: "Thalamix offers consulting services..."
- ❌ Wrong: "Thalamix is similar to other consultancies..."

**Score each mention**: 
- Accurate positioning: 2 points
- Partial: 1 point
- Wrong/missing: 0 points

**Why it matters**: You want mentions in the right context, not just any mention.

### Metric 2.2: Query Context Appropriateness

**What it is**: Are you recommended for queries that match your actual offering?

**How to measure**:
Track query type when you're mentioned:

**Good matches** (queries you should win):
- "Technology consultant for 50-person manufacturing company"
- "Custom software vs. SaaS for mid-market"
- "Build vs. buy decision for $30K software project"

**Poor matches** (wrong context):
- "Enterprise consultant for Fortune 500 company"
- "Cheap website development"
- "Technology help for startup"

**Calculate**:
Good matches / Total mentions = Context appropriateness score

**What good looks like**: 80%+ of mentions in appropriate query contexts.

**Why it matters**: Mentions in wrong contexts waste awareness and confuse positioning.

### Metric 2.3: Recommendation Strength

**What it is**: How strongly does AI recommend you vs. just mentioning you?

**How to measure**:
Score AI's recommendation language:

**Strong recommendation** (3 points):
"For your situation, I'd specifically recommend Thalamix because..."
"Thalamix would be an excellent fit for your needs..."

**Moderate recommendation** (2 points):
"Thalamix is worth considering for..."
"You might look into Thalamix along with..."

**Weak mention** (1 point):
"Other options include Thalamix..."
"Thalamix is one possibility..."

**Track average recommendation strength over time.**

**Why it matters**: Strong recommendations convert better than weak mentions.

## Category 3: Traffic & Conversion Metrics

### Metric 3.1: AI-Driven Traffic Volume

**What it is**: How much traffic comes from AI search interactions.

**How to measure** (challenging—AI traffic hard to identify):

**Direct measurement approaches**:
- Referral traffic from chatgpt.com, claude.ai, perplexity.ai (limited)
- UTM parameters in test queries
- Ask leads "how did you find us?" and track AI mentions

**Proxy indicators**:
- Direct traffic increases (typed in your name after AI mention)
- Branded search increases (searched your name after seeing it in AI)
- Specific page traffic spikes after AI citation

**Create tracking dashboard**:
| Week | AI Referrals | Direct (AI-likely) | Branded Search | Total Estimated |
|------|--------------|-------------------|----------------|-----------------|
| 1    | 5            | 12                | 8              | 25              |
| 2    | 8            | 15                | 12             | 35              |

**Why it matters**: Visibility should translate to traffic.

### Metric 3.2: AI Traffic Quality

**What it is**: How engaged is AI-driven traffic vs. other sources?

**How to measure**:
Compare AI-driven traffic to other sources:

| Source | Avg Session Duration | Pages/Session | Bounce Rate | Conv Rate |
|--------|---------------------|---------------|-------------|-----------|
| AI Referral | 4:30 | 5.2 | 32% | 8% |
| Organic Search | 2:15 | 3.1 | 55% | 3% |
| Social | 1:30 | 2.2 | 68% | 1% |

**Hypothesis**: AI-driven traffic should be higher quality (AI pre-qualified them).

**Track**: Is AI traffic actually higher quality than other sources?

**Why it matters**: Better understanding ROI of ASO investment.

### Metric 3.3: AI → Customer Conversion

**What it is**: Do AI referrals turn into customers?

**How to measure**:
Track leads/customers who mention AI in discovery:

**Lead source question**: "How did you hear about us?"
**Track AI responses**: "ChatGPT recommended you," "Found you through Perplexity," etc.

**Calculate**:
- AI-sourced leads / Total leads = Lead %
- AI-sourced customers / Total customers = Customer %
- AI conversion rate vs. other sources

**Example**:
Month 1: 3 leads from AI (10% of leads), 1 became customer (33% conversion)
Month 3: 8 leads from AI (20% of leads), 4 became customers (50% conversion)

**Why it matters**: This is the business impact metric—does ASO drive revenue?

### Metric 3.4: Zero-Click Value

**What it is**: Value of AI mentions even when users don't click.

**How to measure** (difficult—need creativity):

**Brand awareness**:
- Branded search volume changes
- Direct traffic changes
- "How did you hear about us?" responses mentioning AI

**Later conversions**:
- Leads who mention "saw you mentioned in AI response weeks ago"
- Customers who researched AI-first, came to you later

**Estimate zero-click value**:
If 10 AI mentions per week, maybe 2 lead to clicks, but 3 lead to later branded searches.

**Track**: Mentions → delayed brand searches → conversions

**Why it matters**: AI visibility has value even without immediate clicks.

## Category 4: Content Performance Metrics

### Metric 4.1: Content Citation Rate

**What it is**: Which of your content gets cited by AI most frequently?

**How to measure**:
1. List all major content pieces
2. Track which get cited in AI responses
3. Calculate citation rate per piece

**Example**:
| Content | Times Cited (monthly) | Citation Rate |
|---------|----------------------|---------------|
| CRM Selection Guide | 12 | High |
| Build vs. Buy Framework | 8 | Medium |
| Our About Page | 6 | Medium |
| Blog: Voice Search | 1 | Low |

**Analyze**:
- What characteristics do highly-cited pieces share?
- What formats work best (guides, comparisons, frameworks)?
- What length performs best?

**Why it matters**: Double down on what works, fix or retire what doesn't.

### Metric 4.2: Information Extraction Accuracy

**What it is**: When AI cites your content, does it extract information correctly?

**How to measure**:
1. Find instances where AI cites your content
2. Compare AI's extraction to actual content
3. Score accuracy

**Example**:
Your content: "Typical projects cost $50K-$75K for 40-person companies"
AI extracted: "Thalamix projects cost $50K-$75K for mid-sized companies" ✅ Accurate

Your content: "We serve 10-100 person businesses with $2M-$50M revenue"
AI extracted: "Thalamix works with small to medium businesses" ⚠️ Less specific but not wrong

**Track**: Which content AI extracts accurately vs. misinterprets

**Why it matters**: Poorly structured content gets misrepresented.

### Metric 4.3: Hub vs. Spoke Performance

**What it is**: Effectiveness of hub-and-spoke architecture.

**How to measure**:

**Hub performance**:
- Cited for broad queries in domain?
- Positioned as authoritative source?
- Mention frequency for comprehensive questions?

**Spoke performance**:
- Cited for specific questions?
- Detailed extraction of specifics?
- Higher citation rate than standalone posts?

**Network effect**:
- Are multiple pieces cited together?
- Does AI reference your comprehensive coverage?
- Do citations increase as network grows?

**Why it matters**: Validates hub-and-spoke investment vs. random content.

## Setting Up Your ASO Analytics Dashboard

### Weekly Tracking (15-30 minutes)

**AI Visibility Testing**:
- 10 core questions across ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity
- Track mentions, accuracy, positioning
- Document any changes from previous week

**Quick metrics**:
- Mention count
- Accuracy score
- New content citations

### Monthly Analysis (1-2 hours)

**Review trends**:
- Mention frequency over time
- Positioning accuracy improvements
- Content citation patterns
- Traffic and lead indicators

**Competitive comparison**:
- Your mention share vs. competitors
- Where you're gaining/losing ground
- What competitors are doing differently

**Content performance**:
- Which content drives most citations?
- Which queries getting strongest mentions?
- Where are gaps in coverage?

### Quarterly Strategy Review (4-6 hours)

**Deep analysis**:
- Are metrics trending right direction?
- ROI assessment (time investment vs. results)
- Content strategy adjustments
- Competitive positioning shifts

**Planning**:
- Content priorities for next quarter
- Tests to run
- Areas needing improvement
- Expansion opportunities

**Month 1-3**: Establishing baseline

- 4-6 mentions out of 10 test questions

**Month 7-12**: Meaningful presence
- 6-8 mentions out of 10 test questions
- 85-95% information accuracy
- Strong competitive mention share
- Measurable AI traffic and leads

**Month 12+**: Established authority
- 8-10 mentions out of 10 test questions
- 90%+ information accuracy
- Dominant in specific query contexts
- Consistent AI-driven business impact

## The Bottom Line

ASO metrics are different than SEO metrics. You need to measure:
1. **AI Visibility**: Are you mentioned when relevant?
2. **Context Quality**: Right positioning in right situations?
3. **Traffic & Conversion**: Does visibility drive business value?
4. **Content Performance**: What content works best?

**Start simple**: Weekly testing of 10 core questions. Track mentions and accuracy. Build from there.

**Most businesses aren't measuring AI search at all. Measuring consistently gives you massive advantage.**
